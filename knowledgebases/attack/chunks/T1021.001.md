Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) is a Windows remote interaction protocol that gives
an operator a graphical session, clipboard, and drive redirection on another host.
Adversaries use stolen credentials to log on to additional systems, move tools, and
work interactively rather than through a command line. The connection may come from
a host already compromised inside the perimeter or, when the service is exposed,
from the internet. Successful RDP logons from an unusual source, and RDP sessions
between workstations rather than from administrative jump hosts, are the usual
signal.
