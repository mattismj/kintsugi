An adversary sends a targeted email whose attachment is meant to be opened by the
recipient. The attachment may be a document that runs a macro, an archive, a
shortcut, a script, or a file that fetches further tooling once it is opened.
Delivery depends on convincing a specific person, so the message usually references
something plausible to that person or organization. Detections include
macro-enabled documents and archives from unfamiliar senders, and mail clients or
office applications spawning interpreters and shells.
