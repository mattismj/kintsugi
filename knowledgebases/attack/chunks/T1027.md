Adversaries may make files, scripts, and payloads difficult to discover or
analyze by encrypting, encoding, packing, or otherwise transforming them. Typical
forms are base64 or hex blobs, encrypted or compressed archives, custom packers,
and steganography. The transformation normally has to be reversed at runtime, so
detection tends to land on the decode step rather than on the artifact itself.
High-entropy content that is decoded immediately before execution is the
recurring signal.
