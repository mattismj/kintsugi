Adversaries impair host defenses by disabling, uninstalling, or reconfiguring
security tools: endpoint protection, host firewalls, auditd or event logging, and
monitoring agents. Methods include stopping or deleting services, changing
registry or configuration settings, adding exclusions, and tampering with
signatures. The point is not the tool itself but the silence afterwards, which is
why the modification is often the only observable event. Service stops, exclusion
additions, and logging gaps on one host inside a population that reports normally
are the signal.
