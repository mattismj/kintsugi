Adversaries may use the credentials of existing accounts rather than creating
accounts, exploiting a vulnerability, or dropping a tool. Valid credentials can
come from credential dumping, phishing, a leaked secret, or a default password
that was never changed. Access through a legitimate account blends with normal
activity and often survives remediation aimed at other accounts. Detection rests
on behavioral baselines: logons from an unusual source, impossible travel, access
at an atypical time, and an account reaching resources it never touched before.
