Adversaries may steal data by sending it out over the same channel they already
use for command and control, instead of opening a second connection. Reusing the
established channel is simpler and less conspicuous than standing up a separate
exfiltration path. Data is often bundled, compressed, or encrypted before it is
sent, so the payload looks like ordinary application traffic. Large or unusually
regular outbound transfers on a known command and control session are the signal.
